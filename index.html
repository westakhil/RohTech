🚌 WindowWise VR Prototype

WindowWise is an intelligent passenger-safety demonstration system engineered to eliminate bus window accidents. Through simulated smart perimeter sensors, it monitors passenger postures, aperture limits, and foreign object deployment in real-time, giving the driver immediate preventive command.
