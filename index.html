🚌 WindowWise VR Prototype

WindowWise is a passenger-safety simulation system designed for school buses. Simulated perimeter sensors monitor window postures, aperture limits, and ejected objects in real-time, giving the driver immediate preventative command.

⚠️ Simulation Notice: This is an interactive VR demonstration prototype. Simulated sensor detection algorithms demonstrate the proposed hardware architecture.
Direct VR Dashboard Prominent alert banners and seat indicators right in front of the driver.
Auto Demo Tour One-click automated walkthrough of all 4 safety scenarios and responses.
Floor Teleport Pads Point laser & click glowing floor pads to jump to Seat 2A or back to driver.
Tactile 3D Buttons Reliable debounced controls with audible sirens and PA announcements.
